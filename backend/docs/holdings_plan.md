# Holdings Plan

Holdings are derived from transactions as of the requested date.

- Contributions and dividends add cash.
- Investment buys reduce cash and increase the position's quantity and book value.
- Investment sells increase cash and reduce the position at average cost.
- Account and category distributions aggregate those derived balances.

Snapshot rows remain exportable legacy data, but do not feed the holdings view or analytics.

Live market values require a pricing source; the application currently reports quantities and book values.
